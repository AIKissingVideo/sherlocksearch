## What is Sherlock Search?

Sherlock Search is a browser-based reverse face search workspace built for online identity safety and responsible public-source research. A photo can remain useful when names, usernames, crops, or captions change. Sherlock Search compares visible facial detail with available public indexing and returns possible routes back to source pages.

The result is a set of leads for review—not proof of identity, wrongdoing, or intent. Every promising match should be checked on its original page and corroborated with names, dates, captions, and other independent context.

## What Sherlock Search can help you investigate

- **Profile consistency:** Check whether a public-facing profile photo may appear under another public identity.
- **Photo reuse:** Look for possible copies of your own image across indexed public pages.
- **Possible impersonation:** Find source links that may help you investigate reused or misleading profile photos.
- **Public footprint review:** Understand where a face may be visible across the public web.
- **Source verification:** Move beyond a thumbnail or score and inspect the original page behind a possible match.

## Read evidence, not just scores

Sherlock Search organizes possible visual matches into practical confidence bands:

| Confidence | Label | Recommended action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 85–100% | Strong Match | Open the source and verify the surrounding evidence carefully. |
| 50–84% | Likely Match | Compare facial details and corroborate the page with other context. |
| 0–49% | Unlikely Match | Treat it as a low-priority lead, not a conclusion. |

A similarity score helps prioritize review. It cannot establish a person's legal identity or prove that two profiles belong to the same individual.

## Privacy and safety principles

- **Public pages only:** Sherlock Search does not provide access to private profiles or hidden databases.
- **Private preparation:** Photo setup stays in your browser before the search begins.
- **Limited retention:** Uploaded search media may be retained for up to 24 hours.
- **No silent subscription:** No card or recurring subscription is required during the current free-access period.
- **Possible matches, not verdicts:** Always verify the source before reporting, accusing, or acting on a result.
- **Responsible use:** Use Sherlock Search only for lawful purposes. Never use it for harassment, stalking, intimidation, or unlawful surveillance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sherlock Search free?

Sherlock Search is currently free. The site states that no card, subscription, or registration is required during free access. Availability may change, so check the website for the latest terms.

### Does Sherlock Search access private accounts?

No. It works with available public indexing. Private, deleted, blocked, or unindexed content may not appear.

### Are visual matches proof of identity?

No. A visual similarity score is only a lead. Open the original source and compare independent context before reaching a conclusion.

### What happens if no useful match appears?

A zero-match or low-quality result does not prove that a face is absent from the internet. The relevant page may be private, removed, unindexed, blocked, or based on a visually different image.

### How long is uploaded media retained?

The current website states that search media may be retained for up to 24 hours.
